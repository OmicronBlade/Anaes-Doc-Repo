# Third-party material

The electronic versions in this repository reproduce the layout and wording of forms owned by other
organisations so that they can be completed on screen and printed. **That material is not covered by
this repository's MIT or CC BY-NC-SA licences** and remains the property of its owners. It is
reproduced for use within the New Somerset Hospital (NSH) Department of Anaesthesia; anyone else
wishing to reuse it should obtain permission from the owner.

| Material | Location | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Patient Safety Incident (PSI) Reporting Form | `forms/psi-report.*` | Western Cape Government: Health and Wellness |
| Injury on Duty pack (occupational exposure / PEP forms) | `forms/injury-on-duty.*` | New Somerset Hospital, Occupational Health & Safety |
| NHLS Clinical Pathology request form (P02A1422) | `forms/shared/nhls-request.*`, Injury on Duty pages 6–7 | National Health Laboratory Service |
| COIDA First Medical Report (W.Cl.4) | Injury on Duty pack | Compensation Fund, Department of Employment and Labour |
| C/S Prescription Chart, Induction of Labour Form, PCA Prescription | `forms/cs-prescription.*`, `forms/induction-of-labour.*`, `forms/pca-prescription.*` | New Somerset Hospital (departmental forms) |
| Western Cape Government logo | `assets/img/wcg-logo.jpg` | Western Cape Government |
| New Somerset Hospital crest | `assets/img/nsh-crest.jpg` | New Somerset Hospital |
| NHLS logo | `assets/img/nhls-logo.png` | National Health Laboratory Service |
| Department of Employment and Labour emblem | `assets/img/dol-labour.png` | Department of Employment and Labour |

Paths are relative to `anaesthesia-forms/`. The code that turns these forms into fillable pages is
MIT-licensed; the forms' own wording, layout and branding are not.

## Cited sources

The regional anaesthesia consent forms and leaflets cite, but do not reproduce:

1. South African Society of Regional Anaesthesia (SASRA). SASA guidelines for regional anaesthesia in
   South Africa 2016. Johannesburg: SASRA; 2016.
2. McCombe K, Bogod D. Regional anaesthesia: risk, consent and complications. Anaesthesia.
   2021;76(Suppl 1):18-26. https://doi.org/10.1111/anae.15246
3. Burger R, Smit M, Plenge U, Gibbs MW. Informed consent for peripheral nerve blocks at a tertiary
   level hospital in South Africa: a quality improvement project. South Afr J Anaesth Analg.
   2024;30(2):62-6. https://doi.org/10.36303/SAJAA.3061
